## Contract Intake Form: Recommended Fields

### 1. Requester and Ownership
- Requester name, title, department
- Business owner (accountable for the relationship after signature)
- Executive sponsor, if value or risk exceeds a set threshold
- Cost center or budget code
- Date submitted

### 2. Counterparty
- Full legal name of the counterparty entity
- Entity type and jurisdiction of formation
- Address and primary contact
- New or existing counterparty
- Government, state-owned or public-sector entity? (Y/N)
- Related party, affiliate or known conflict of interest? (Y/N)
- Sanctions or restricted-party screening completed? (Y/N or N/A)

### 3. Contract Basics
- Contract type (NDA, MSA, SOW, purchase, sales, license, lease, partnership, other)
- New, renewal, amendment or termination
- Whose paper: company template or counterparty
- Parent agreement reference, if this is a SOW, order form or amendment
- Draft and prior versions attached

### 4. Commercial Terms
- Description of goods, services or rights exchanged
- Total contract value and annual value
- Currency and payment terms
- Start date, term length, renewal or auto-renewal
- Budget approved? (Y/N)

### 5. Risk Screening (Y/N triggers that route to specialists)
- Personal data collected, accessed or processed?
- Confidential, sensitive or regulated data shared?
- IP created, assigned or licensed? Contractor-developed deliverables?
- Software, SaaS, AI tools or open-source components?
- Cross-border performance, data transfer or export-controlled items?
- Interaction with government officials or intermediaries/agents?
- Exclusivity, non-compete, MFN or volume commitments?
- Non-standard liability, indemnity or warranty terms requested?
- Insurance requirements on either side?
- Subcontractors or third-party access to company systems or sites?
- On-site work or safety-relevant activity?

### 6. Timing
- Requested completion date
- Reason for urgency
- Hard external deadlines (e.g., bid date, go-live, expiring offer)

### 7. Approvals and Signature
- Approvals required under the company's delegation of authority
- Proposed signatory, with authority confirmed
- Procurement or vendor onboarding status
- Security, privacy or finance review status, where triggered

### 8. Post-Signature Administration
- Repository location for the executed copy
- Obligation owner(s)
- Renewal, notice and termination-notice dates
- Key deliverables or milestones to track

### 9. Legal / Contracts Team Use Only
- Matter or tracking number
- Assigned reviewer
- Risk tier (e.g., standard / elevated / escalated)
- Deviations from standard positions
- Status and cycle-time tracking
- Internal notes

## Design Tips
- **Keep the requester burden low.** Use dropdowns and Y/N fields. Use conditional logic so risk questions expand only when triggered.
- **Make triggers route automatically.** For example, personal data goes to Privacy, software goes to Security, and government involvement goes to Compliance.
- **Tie approvals to your actual delegation of authority.** The form should reflect it, not invent thresholds.
- **Keep Section 9 separate** from the requester-facing view.
- **Don't invite requesters to write legal conclusions** in free text (e.g., "this is low risk"). Ask for facts instead.
- **Capture data for metrics.** Cycle time, volume by type and deviation frequency let you report on the function later.
