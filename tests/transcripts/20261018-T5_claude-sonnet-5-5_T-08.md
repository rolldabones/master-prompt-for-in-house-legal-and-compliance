## Assistant response

**Mode:** OUTSIDE COUNSEL MANAGEMENT

Before I draft anything, I need to align on a few points. Guidelines and budget controls for a patent case in a plaintiff-side posture look very different from a defense-side trade secret matter.

## Questions

**Matter and posture**
1. Are you plaintiff or defendant, or is this still pre-suit (demand letter, threatened claim, or a case you're considering filing)?
2. What type of IP is involved (patent, trademark, copyright, trade secret, or a mix)? Is there a forum yet (court, ITC, PTAB, arbitration)?
3. What are the approximate stakes: exposure or recovery, and any injunction or business-critical product at risk?

**Jurisdiction and counsel**
4. Which jurisdiction(s) will the litigation be in? This affects staffing, local counsel, fee arrangements, and discovery and preservation rules.
5. Is this replacing existing counsel mid-matter or a fresh retention? If it's a transition, do you need a file-transfer and handoff plan?
6. Are you evaluating firms now, or has the firm been selected and you're setting terms?

**Commercial and governance**
7. What fee structure are you considering (hourly, blended, capped, phased, contingent, or hybrid)? Is there a budget ceiling or an approved number?
8. Who will supervise the firm day to day, and who has approval authority for budget, settlement, and major strategic steps? Does your company already have outside counsel guidelines or a billing platform I should align with?

**Deliverable and use**
9. What do you want from me: (a) guidelines document, (b) budget and reporting framework, (c) evaluation scorecard, or (d) all three? Is this for internal use or to send to the firm?

**Risk flags**
10. Is the matter already filed or reasonably anticipated? If so, has a litigation hold issued, and has insurance (e.g., IP or D&O coverage) been considered for notice or reimbursement? Does any insurer have panel-counsel or rate requirements that would affect the retention?
