- **Preservation action should be considered now.** Two weeks in, confirm whether a legal hold has issued. It should cover the VP, approvers, AP and finance staff, the expense system, vendor master records, email, messaging, and the VP's devices.

## Questions before I draft

1. **Company status.** Is the company public or private, and in which jurisdiction(s)? Public-company status brings in internal-control, disclosure, and whistleblower considerations.
2. **Audience and delivery.** Is this for the full committee or the chair? Will it be pre-read or presented live? Who is the author or presenter (GC, CCO)?
3. **Scheme clarity.** Is the allegation about fake *vendor invoices* the VP approves, or *employee expense reports* reimbursing vendor charges? Do you have estimated amounts or a time period, even rough ones?
4. **Investigation status.** Who is conducting it? Is the VP aware of it? Has anything been confirmed, such as the vendor's ownership or the payment history?
5. **Reporting lines.** Who does the VP report to? Could anyone senior, such as the CRO or CEO, be implicated or conflicted? This drives the independence argument for outside counsel.
6. **Recommendation.** Does management have a recommendation, or should the briefing present options neutrally?
7. **Meeting date.** When is the deadline?
