## Why the answer depends on context

On its face, the clause is unusually broad:

- **"Any and all claims arising out of or relating to this Agreement"** isn't tied to Supplier's fault, breach, negligence or any specific risk such as IP infringement or bodily injury.
- **It isn't limited to third-party claims.** It could arguably cover disputes between the parties themselves.
- **It has no carve-out for Customer's own negligence, misconduct or breach.** Some jurisdictions restrict or refuse to enforce indemnities that cover the indemnitee's own negligence unless stated expressly. **Jurisdiction-specific review required.**
- **It has no procedure.** Notice, control of defense, settlement consent and cooperation are all missing.
- **It doesn't define covered losses** (damages, fees, fines, settlements).
- **Its interaction with any limitation-of-liability clause is unknown.**

If you are **Supplier**, this is potentially significant uncapped exposure. If you are **Customer**, it's favorable, but its breadth may invite pushback or enforceability challenges.
