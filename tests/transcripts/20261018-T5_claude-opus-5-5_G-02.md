## Purpose of the Draft

This is a first-draft, balanced mutual NDA for early-stage discussions between two companies evaluating a software partnership, such as integration, co-development, reseller or technology licensing. It protects both sides' information during evaluation. It does not grant rights, create a partnership or commit either party to a deal.

## Key Assumptions

1. Both parties are business entities, not individuals. They are of roughly comparable bargaining power.
2. US-based parties. Governing law is a placeholder (Delaware) pending confirmation.
3. The exchange may include technical information, product roadmaps, pricing and business plans. Source code may be exchanged but isn't expected, so an optional heightened-protection clause is included.
4. No exchange of personal data is intended.
5. The NDA covers evaluation only. Any commercial deal will be a separate definitive agreement.
6. Neither party is assumed to be publicly traded. A securities clause is bracketed in case one is.
7. A 2-year disclosure period, 3-year confidentiality tail and indefinite protection for trade secrets are used as common market positions. **Requires current legal verification.**

## MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [EFFECTIVE DATE] (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**[PARTY A LEGAL NAME]**, a [STATE/COUNTRY] [ENTITY TYPE], with its principal place of business at [ADDRESS] ("**Party A**"); and

**[PARTY B LEGAL NAME]**, a [STATE/COUNTRY] [ENTITY TYPE], with its principal place of business at [ADDRESS] ("**Party B**").

Party A and Party B are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to evaluate a potential business relationship concerning [BRIEF DESCRIPTION, e.g., "the integration of Party A's [PRODUCT] with Party B's [PRODUCT]"] (the "**Purpose**"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. This Agreement governs that disclosure.

### 2. Definitions

2.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

2.2 "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other Party (the "**Receiving Party**"), whether before or after the Effective Date, in any form. This includes oral, written, electronic and visual disclosures, and any disclosure through access to systems, demonstrations or facilities. Information qualifies as Confidential Information if it is (a) marked or identified as confidential at the time of disclosure, or (b) information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

Confidential Information includes, without limitation:
- software, source code, object code, algorithms, APIs, architecture and technical documentation;
- product roadmaps, specifications and designs;
- trade secrets and know-how;
- pricing, cost and financial information;
- customer, supplier and partner information;
- business and marketing plans; and
- the existence, status and terms of the Parties' discussions.

2.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, contractors, professional advisors (including legal counsel, accountants and financial advisors) and [actual or prospective financing sources]. In each case, the person must have a need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

### 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by competent evidence:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party without restriction before receipt from the Disclosing Party;

(c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction and without breach of any obligation owed to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

### 4. Obligations of the Receiving Party

4.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party will use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

4.2 **Non-disclosure.** The Receiving Party will not disclose Confidential Information to anyone other than its Representatives. Each Representative must (a) need to know the information for the Purpose and (b) be bound by written or professional confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement.

4.3 **Standard of care.** The Receiving Party will protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own information of similar sensitivity, and in no event less than reasonable care.

4.4 **Responsibility for Representatives.** Each Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

4.5 **Notice of unauthorized disclosure.** The Receiving Party will promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing upon becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information. It will also reasonably cooperate to mitigate the effects.

4.6 **No reverse engineering.** The Receiving Party will not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any software, prototypes or other tangible items provided by the Disclosing Party, except as expressly authorized in writing.

### 5. Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by law, regulation, court order or governmental authority to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party will, to the extent legally permitted:

(a) give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy;

(b) reasonably cooperate with the Disclosing Party, at the Disclosing Party's expense; and

(c) disclose only the portion of Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

Disclosure under this Section is not a breach of this Agreement.

### 6. [OPTIONAL] Source Code and Highly Sensitive Information

[If either Party discloses source code or information designated in writing as "Highly Confidential," the Receiving Party will:

(a) restrict access to named individuals approved in advance in writing by the Disclosing Party;

(b) access the material only in a manner or environment specified by the Disclosing Party;

(c) not copy, download or transmit the material except as expressly permitted in writing; and

(d) maintain a log of persons who accessed the material and provide it on request.]

### 7. Personal Data

The Parties do not intend to exchange personal data under this Agreement. Neither Party will disclose personal data to the other unless the Parties first execute a data processing or data sharing agreement that complies with applicable data protection law.

### 8. Ownership; No License

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in any Confidential Information, patent, copyright, trade secret, trademark or other intellectual property, whether by implication, estoppel or otherwise, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

### 9. No Warranty

ALL CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION IS PROVIDED "AS IS." NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY REPRESENTATION OR WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, AS TO ITS ACCURACY, COMPLETENESS, PERFORMANCE OR NON-INFRINGEMENT. Neither Party will be liable for the other Party's use of Confidential Information except as expressly set out in this Agreement.

### 10. No Obligation; Independent Activities

10.1 **No obligation to proceed.** This Agreement does not obligate either Party to disclose any particular information, to continue discussions or to enter into any further agreement. Any definitive agreement must be in a separate signed writing.

10.2 **Independent activities.** Each Party acknowledges that the other may currently or in the future develop, acquire or market products or services similar to or competitive with those of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement restricts either Party from doing so, provided that it does not use or disclose the other Party's Confidential Information in breach of this Agreement.

### 11. Return or Destruction

11.1 **Obligation.** Within [thirty (30)] days after the Disclosing Party's written request, or after termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party will return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its possession or control. If requested, it will certify the return or destruction in writing.

11.2 **Exceptions.** The Receiving Party may retain:

(a) copies required by law, regulation or a bona fide internal document retention policy; and

(b) copies stored in automatic electronic backups that are not readily accessible in the ordinary course.

Any retained copies remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are retained.

### 12. Term and Survival

12.1 **Term.** This Agreement covers disclosures made during the period from the Effective Date until the earlier of (a) [two (2)] years after the Effective Date, or (b) termination by either Party on [thirty (30)] days' written notice.

12.2 **Survival.** The Receiving Party's obligations for Confidential Information disclosed during the term survive for [three (3)] years after expiration or termination. For any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law, the obligations survive for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

### 13. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. The Disclosing Party is therefore entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief, without the need to post a bond [to the extent permitted by law]. This relief is in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

### 14. Export Compliance

Each Party will comply with all applicable export control and sanctions laws in connection with Confidential Information. Neither Party will export, re-export or transfer any technical data received under this Agreement in violation of those laws.

### 15. [OPTIONAL — USE IF EITHER PARTY IS PUBLICLY TRADED] Securities Laws

[Each Party acknowledges that it is aware that applicable securities laws restrict any person who possesses material non-public information about a publicly traded company from (a) purchasing or selling that company's securities, or (b) communicating that information to others who may do so. Each Party will advise its Representatives accordingly.]

### 16. General

16.1 **Governing law; venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of [the State of Delaware], without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in [COUNTY, STATE]. Either Party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

16.2 **No publicity.** Neither Party will publicly announce or disclose the existence or subject matter of the Parties' discussions without the other Party's prior written consent.

16.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent. However, either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of all or substantially all of its assets or business to which this Agreement relates, provided the successor agrees in writing to be bound. Any assignment in violation of this Section is void.

16.4 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties concerning its subject matter. It supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements and understandings on that subject.

16.5 **Amendment; waiver.** Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right.

16.6 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions will remain in full force.

16.7 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses set out below or as later updated by notice.

16.8 **Relationship.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or fiduciary relationship.

16.9 **Counterparts; electronic signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument. Electronic signatures are valid and binding.

## Negotiable Points (Likely Counterparty Asks)

| Issue | Draft position | Common counter-positions |
|---|---|---|
| Marking requirement | Marked **or** reasonably understood as confidential | Marking required, with oral disclosures confirmed in writing within 30 days. This is easier to administer but riskier for the discloser. |
| Confidentiality tail | 3 years, with trade secrets protected indefinitely | 2 to 5 years. Some parties want a flat term with no trade-secret carve-out. |
| Residuals clause | **Not included** | Larger tech counterparties often request the right to use information retained in unaided memory. This significantly weakens protection, so resist it or narrow it heavily. |
| Non-solicitation of employees | Not included | Often requested in partnership NDAs. Enforceability varies by jurisdiction. **Jurisdiction-specific review required.** |
| Independent development (§10.2) | Included | Disclosers of sensitive technology may try to narrow it or require documentation of independent development. |
| No reverse engineering | Included | Usually accepted. Watch for interaction with open-source license terms. |
| Feedback / suggestions | Not addressed | Counterparties may seek ownership of or a license to feedback. Better handled in the definitive agreement. |
| Injunctive relief without bond | Included, qualified | Some courts disregard bond waivers. Usually low-friction. |
| Assignment on change of control | Permitted | A competitor-acquirer concern may prompt a counterparty to seek a consent right. |

## Review Points Before Use

1. **Check against company template and policy.** Your organization may have a standard NDA, a signature authority matrix or required clauses. **Unknown/Insufficient data.** If a standard form exists, use it or reconcile this draft against it.
2. **Confirm governing law and enforceability.** Remedies, bond waivers, indefinite trade-secret protection and any non-solicitation added later can vary by jurisdiction. **Requires current legal verification.**
3. **Cross-border use.** If either party is outside the US, review governing law, data protection, export control and enforceability. **Jurisdiction-specific review required.**
4. **Source code.** If source code will be exchanged, keep Section 6. Also consider whether a short standalone source-code access protocol is warranted.
5. **Personal data.** If any customer, user or employee data may be shared, a DPA is needed first. Do not rely on this NDA for that.
6. **Public company status.** If either party is listed, keep Section 15 and brief your team on MNPI handling.
7. **Signature authority.** Confirm the signatory is authorized under your delegation of authority.
8. **Prior disclosures.** If information has already been exchanged, the definition captures pre-Effective Date disclosures. Confirm this is intended.
