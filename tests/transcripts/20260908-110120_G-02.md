## C. DRAFT — MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

> **[DRAFT — FOR INTERNAL REVIEW — NOT FOR EXECUTION WITHOUT COUNSEL REVIEW]**

**MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of **[DATE]** (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]**, a **[STATE/COUNTRY]** **[entity type]** with its principal place of business at **[ADDRESS]** ("**[Company Short Name]**"); and

**[COUNTERPARTY LEGAL NAME]**, a **[STATE/COUNTRY]** **[entity type]** with its principal place of business at **[ADDRESS]** ("**[Counterparty Short Name]**").

Each of the above is a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**." A Party disclosing Confidential Information is the "**Discloser**"; a Party receiving it is the "**Recipient**." Each Party may act in both capacities.

**RECITALS**

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship involving **[a potential software partnership, including possible integration, joint development, reselling and/or co-marketing of their respective software products]** (the "**Purpose**"), and in connection therewith may disclose certain confidential and proprietary information to one another.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Parties agree as follows:

---

**1. Definition of Confidential Information**

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of Discloser to Recipient in connection with the Purpose, in any form (written, oral, visual, electronic, or by access to systems or facilities), that is either (a) marked or identified as confidential or proprietary at or about the time of disclosure, or (b) that a reasonable person in the Recipient's position would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information or the circumstances of disclosure.

1.2 Confidential Information includes, without limitation: software, software architecture, APIs, data models, algorithms, technical specifications, documentation, designs, product and technology roadmaps, security and infrastructure information, testing and performance data, unreleased features, business and marketing plans, pricing and cost information, customer and supplier identities and requirements, financial information, personnel information, and the terms and existence of the Parties' discussions.

1.3 **[Optional — include only if source code will actually be disclosed:** Source code disclosed hereunder shall be treated as Confidential Information of the highest sensitivity, shall be disclosed only pursuant to a separate written protocol agreed by the Parties, and shall not be copied, compiled, executed, or disclosed to any person other than a limited number of named individuals agreed in advance in writing.**]**

1.4 The fact that the Parties are engaged in discussions concerning the Purpose, and the nature and status of those discussions, shall be Confidential Information of both Parties.

**2. Exclusions**

Confidential Information does not include information that Recipient can demonstrate by contemporaneous written records:

(a) was lawfully in Recipient's possession without obligation of confidentiality before receipt from Discloser;
(b) is or becomes publicly available through no act or omission of Recipient or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;
(c) is lawfully received by Recipient from a third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation owed to Discloser and without restriction on disclosure; or
(d) is independently developed by Recipient or its Affiliates without use of or reference to Discloser's Confidential Information.

**3. Obligations of the Recipient**

3.1 **Use restriction.** Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose, including without limitation no competitive, product development, procurement leverage, or commercial advantage purpose.

3.2 **Non-disclosure.** Recipient shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party except as expressly permitted by this Agreement.

3.3 **Standard of care.** Recipient shall protect Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care, including commercially reasonable administrative, physical and technical safeguards.

3.4 **Permitted recipients.** Recipient may disclose Confidential Information only to its and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, and professional advisors **[and, with Discloser's prior written consent, contractors]** who (a) have a genuine need to know for the Purpose, and (b) are bound by written or professional obligations of confidentiality and use restriction no less protective than those in this Agreement (each, a "**Representative**"). Recipient shall be liable for any act or omission of its Representatives that would constitute a breach of this Agreement if committed by Recipient.

3.5 **Notice of unauthorized disclosure.** Recipient shall promptly notify Discloser in writing upon becoming aware of any unauthorized use, disclosure, loss, or compromise of Confidential Information, and shall reasonably cooperate with Discloser to mitigate the effects thereof.

3.6 **No reverse engineering.** Recipient shall not, and shall not permit any third party to, reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive the source code, structure, or underlying ideas of any software, prototype, sample, or object code made available by Discloser, except to the extent this restriction is unenforceable under applicable law.

3.7 **[Optional — benchmarking:** Recipient shall not conduct or publish any benchmark, performance, or security test of Discloser's software or systems without Discloser's prior written consent.**]**

**4. Compelled Disclosure**

If Recipient or its Representatives are required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or the rules of any securities exchange or governmental or regulatory authority to disclose Confidential Information, Recipient may do so provided that, to the extent legally permitted and practicable, Recipient (a) gives Discloser prompt written notice sufficient to allow Discloser to seek a protective order or other remedy, (b) reasonably cooperates with Discloser (at Discloser's expense) in such efforts, and (c) discloses only that portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose and uses reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment for it. Notice is not required where prohibited by law or in connection with routine supervisory examinations by a regulator having jurisdiction over Recipient.

Nothing in this Agreement prohibits or restricts either Party or any individual from reporting possible violations of law to, or participating in an investigation by, any governmental agency or regulatory authority, or from making other disclosures protected under applicable whistleblower laws, without notice to the other Party.

**5. No License; Ownership**

5.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of Discloser. No license or other right, express or implied, under any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right is granted by this Agreement or by any disclosure hereunder.

5.2 Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to disclose any particular information, and each Party may cease disclosure at any time.

5.3 **[Optional — Feedback:** If Recipient provides suggestions or feedback regarding Discloser's products or services, Discloser may use such feedback without restriction or obligation, provided Discloser does not identify Recipient as the source without consent.**]**

**6. No Obligation; No Exclusivity; No Representation**

6.1 This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into any further agreement, transaction, or business relationship, and does not create any joint venture, partnership, agency, or fiduciary relationship.

6.2 Nothing in this Agreement restricts either Party from independently pursuing, developing, marketing, acquiring, or entering into agreements with third parties regarding products, services, or opportunities similar to or competitive with those contemplated by the Purpose, provided it does not breach this Agreement.

6.3 Each Party acknowledges that Confidential Information is provided "AS IS," and neither Party makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to its accuracy, completeness, or fitness for any purpose. Any such representations will be made, if at all, in a subsequent definitive written agreement.

**7. Term; Duration of Obligations**

7.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for **[TWO (2)]** years, unless earlier terminated by either Party upon **[thirty (30)]** days' prior written notice.
