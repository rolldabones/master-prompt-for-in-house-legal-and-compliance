## DRAFT LEGAL HOLD NOTICE

**PRIVILEGED & CONFIDENTIAL – LEGAL HOLD – DO NOT FORWARD**

**To:** [Custodian names / distribution list]
**From:** [Name], [General Counsel / Legal Department]
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Legal Hold – Mandatory Preservation of Documents and Data – [Plant Name] / [Former Employee Name]
**Action required:** Read, comply immediately, and return the attached acknowledgment by [date].

**1. Why you are receiving this notice**

The Company has received a communication from a former employee, [Name], concerning the end of their employment and workplace safety at [Plant Name]. The Company reasonably anticipates that legal or regulatory proceedings may follow. The Company is therefore legally required to preserve all potentially relevant information. You are receiving this notice because you may have such information. Receiving it does not mean you did anything wrong.

**2. What you must do, effective immediately**

Preserve, and do not delete, discard, alter, overwrite, or move, any documents or data described below, wherever they are located. This overrides all Company records-retention schedules and auto-delete settings. Do not wait for IT to act.

**3. Time period**

[Date] to the present, and continuing. Anything you create or receive after today that fits the categories below must also be preserved.

**4. What to preserve**

*A. The former employee*
- Personnel, payroll, performance, discipline, and complaint records
- Separation and severance documents, including drafts, negotiations, and approvals
- Any communications with, or about, [Name], including the recent email to [CEO] and all replies, forwards, and related discussions
- Any concerns, reports, or complaints [Name] raised during employment, in any form

*B. Safety at [Plant Name]*
- Incident, injury, illness, and near-miss reports and logs
- Inspection, audit, and risk-assessment records
- Maintenance, repair, and equipment records
- Safety policies, procedures, training records, and committee minutes
- Corrective-action records and work orders
- Complaints or concerns about safety from any employee, contractor, or third party
- Communications with any regulator, insurer, auditor, or consultant about plant safety

*C. Internal communications and decisions*
- Communications about the matters above, including by executives, HR, operations, EHS, legal, and finance
- Any analysis or discussion of how to respond to the former employee's communication (see Section 6 on privilege)

**5. Where to look**

Preserve in every format and location, including:
- Email and calendars
- Teams, Slack, text, WhatsApp, and other messaging, whether on Company or personal devices if used for work
- Shared drives, SharePoint, cloud storage, and local or desktop files
- Laptops, phones, tablets, and removable media
- Paper files, notebooks, and handwritten notes
- Safety, maintenance, HR, and payroll systems
- Photos, video (including plant camera footage), and voicemail

If you are unsure whether something is covered, preserve it and ask Legal.

**6. Handling and confidentiality**

- Do not discuss this matter with anyone outside the Company, including the former employee, media, or social media.
- Do not contact the former employee about this matter. Refer any inquiry from them, the press, or any agency to [Legal contact].
- Do not forward this notice or discuss it beyond those who need to know.
- Do not create new commentary, speculation, or analysis about the matter in email or messaging, unless counsel asks you to.
- Do not conduct your own investigation, and do not alter, "clean up," or backfill any safety or HR records.
- Continue normal business operations and normal, accurate record-keeping. This notice does not require any change to safety practices and does not discourage anyone from reporting safety concerns through the usual channels.

**7. If you leave your role, change devices, or know of other relevant people or locations**

Tell Legal before any device is replaced, wiped, or returned, and before any departure. Tell us about anyone else who may hold relevant information.

**8. Duration**

This hold stays in effect until Legal releases it in writing. Failure to comply may expose the Company and individuals to serious consequences, and may result in disciplinary action.

**Questions:** [Name, phone, email]

**ACKNOWLEDGMENT**
I have read and understand this Legal Hold Notice and will comply. I have identified the following additional locations or people who may have relevant information: ______
Name: ______  Title: ______  Signature: ______  Date: ______
