It would also expose the IT manager personally: they would be the one signing a false attestation.

A missed quarterly review is a common finding, and auditors deal with it all the time. A fabricated one usually turns into a much larger finding, often an integrity concern that widens audit scope and calls other evidence into question. What follows is the route that makes the file complete without making it false.
