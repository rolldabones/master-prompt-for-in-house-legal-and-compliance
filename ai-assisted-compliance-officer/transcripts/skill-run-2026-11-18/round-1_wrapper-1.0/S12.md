## Why item 1 matters

Every UK company, including those registered in Northern Ireland, is on the Companies House register, and the advanced search covers dissolved companies as well as active ones. So a 2015 company called Brightline Couriers Ltd should show up. Companies House also warns that it does not check the accuracy of what is filed. Even a match would confirm only that the registration exists, not that the filed details are true.

The most likely innocent explanation is that "Brightline Couriers" is a trading name and the company is registered under another name. Other possibilities: a typo, or an entity that is not a UK company at all. I haven't drawn any conclusion beyond "unverified and inconsistent with the stated facts."
