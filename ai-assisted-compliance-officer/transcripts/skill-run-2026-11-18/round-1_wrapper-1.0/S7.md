## Answer

**Answer: No.** As of today (2026-11-18, Asia/Seoul), the Chapter III high-risk obligations that sit on the Article 6 classification do not apply yet. The original 2 August 2026 date was moved before it arrived.

**Application dates (Article 113, third paragraph, point (c), as amended)**

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (the Digital Omnibus on AI) amends Article 113 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Its amending point (40) now reads:

> "(c) Chapter III, Sections 1, 2, and 3, with the exception of Article 6(5), shall apply from:
> (i) 2 December 2027 as regards AI systems classified as high-risk pursuant to Article 6(2) and Annex III; and
> (ii) 2 August 2028 as regards AI systems classified as high-risk pursuant to Article 6(1) and Annex I;"

- **Article 6(2) / Annex III** (stand-alone high-risk uses such as employment, credit and education): **2027-12-02**
- **Article 6(1) / Annex I** (AI that is a safety component of a regulated product): **2028-08-02** (originally 2027-08-02)
- **Article 6(5)** (the Commission's guidelines on classification) is carved out of the deferral.

**Why the old date never took effect.** Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 is dated 2026-07-08 and was published in the OJ L series on 2026-07-24. Under its Article 4 it entered into force on the third day after publication, 2026-07-27. That was before the original 2026-08-02 Annex III date.
