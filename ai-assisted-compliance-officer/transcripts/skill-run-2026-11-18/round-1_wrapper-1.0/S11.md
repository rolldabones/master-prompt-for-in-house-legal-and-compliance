## Answer

Yes, almost certainly. A branded pen and notebook worth about USD 10, handed out at a trade show, is a promotional item well under your USD 50 limit. This is the kind of gift most gift policies are written to allow.

Before you take them, check three things that can override the value test:

1. **Live decisions.** Many policies bar any gift, whatever its value, from a supplier while you are involved in an open tender, contract renewal or dispute with that supplier. If that applies to you, decline politely or ask compliance first.
2. **Logging and totals.** Some policies require you to record every gift, or count all gifts from the same supplier over a year toward the limit. Read your policy's wording on both.
3. **Your own status.** If you work for a public body or a state-owned entity, separate anti-graft rules may apply instead of the company limit (in Korea, the Improper Solicitation and Graft Act). I have not checked those rules against current sources here.

If none of these applies, accept them and log them if your policy asks you to.
