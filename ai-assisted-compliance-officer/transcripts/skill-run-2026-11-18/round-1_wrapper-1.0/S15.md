## 2. Why the UK misses still bear on the EU conclusion

- **Shared procedure.** If the EU entities run the same procedure, screening tool, list feeds or central team as the UK entity, the cause of the UK misses could be present in the EU too (for example a list-update lag, fuzzy-match thresholds, a gap in onboarding triggers or a manual workaround). Until the root cause is known I'd limit the EU conclusion to design and implementation, and qualify any view on operating effectiveness.
- **Separate procedure.** If the UK runs its own procedure, the EU conclusion can stand alone, and the scope line above is enough.
- **Escalation.** Missed screenings may mean a sanctions exposure, and under UK and possibly EU rules that can carry reporting duties that run on their own clock. Whoever owns the UK matter should confirm, with external counsel, whether any screened-late counterparty was a true match and whether any report is due. ⚠ External counsel is recommended because regulator involvement is possible. This is a flag to you only. I haven't contacted anyone.
- If you have a personal stake in how the UK finding is reported, the decision on how the paper treats it should go to someone independent, such as the audit or risk committee chair or the General Counsel.
