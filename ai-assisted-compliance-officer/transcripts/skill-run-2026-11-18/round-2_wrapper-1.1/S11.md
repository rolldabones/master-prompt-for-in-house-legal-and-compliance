## Answer

Very likely yes. A branded pen and notebook worth about USD 10 is the textbook nominal promotional item, well under your USD 50 limit. I have not seen your policy, so check four points in it first:

1. **Timing.** Many policies bar any supplier gift, however small, while that supplier is bidding in a tender you are involved in or while a renewal, negotiation or dispute with it is open. If so, decline politely.
2. **Logging.** Some policies require every supplier gift to be recorded in a gifts register, even below the approval limit. If yours does, log it.
3. **Aggregation.** Check whether USD 50 is per gift or per supplier per year. It only matters if this supplier has already given you other things.
4. **Your role.** If you work in procurement or evaluate this supplier, your policy may set a lower limit or none at all for you.

If none of these applies, take them. Giveaways offered to every visitor at a trade show booth are generally the lowest-risk category, because nothing about them is aimed at you personally or tied to a decision you make.

One caveat: this answer is about your company's internal policy, not the law. If your employer is a government body or state-owned entity, public-sector gift rules may be stricter than your policy suggests, so confirm with your compliance team.
