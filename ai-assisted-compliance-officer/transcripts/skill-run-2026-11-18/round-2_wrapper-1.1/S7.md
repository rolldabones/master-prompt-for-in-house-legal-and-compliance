## Answer

No. The high-risk classification rules and obligations in Chapter III, Sections 1 to 3 (which include Article 6) have not started applying. The Digital Omnibus on AI pushed the dates back before the original 2 August 2026 date arrived.

**Application dates (Article 113, third paragraph, point (c), Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended):**

- **Annex III high-risk systems (Article 6(2)):** from 2027-12-02
- **Annex I product-safety high-risk systems (Article 6(1)):** from 2028-08-02
- Article 6(5) (the Commission's classification guidelines) is carved out of the delay.

**The amending act.** Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), dated 2026-07-08, is enacted and was published in OJ L, 2026/1744, 2026-07-24. Under its Article 4 it entered into force on the third day after publication. Its point (40) rewrites Article 113(c) with the dates above. I opened and read the operative text in the official Official Journal PDF on EUR-Lex ([OJ L 2026/1744](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:L_202601744)).
